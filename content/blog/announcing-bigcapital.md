We're building core accounting software with an enjoyable modern user experience, efficient financial statements and KPIs, and more workflow automation.

Managing the finances of any organization is a crucial activity for business owners and accountants. Most accountants automate repetitive tasks with software covering areas such as A/R, A/P, transaction categorization, syncing transactions from different apps, and closing the books to ensure accurate financial statements.

This is why accounting software is still one of the oldest and most popular types of software. Many leading solutions on the market were developed over a decade ago using older technologies, and core accounting has not evolved significantly since then, despite tremendous advances in A/R, A/P, and payroll technology.

## What makes Bigcapital different

- **Deliver an exceptional user experience.** We prioritize application performance and UI/UX. Accounting software is used multiple times a day by business owners and accountants who support other businesses, so it should be simple and fast to navigate.

- **Better financial statements, reports, and analysis KPIs.** The best accounting software gives executives a clear picture of the organization's financial position, so they can make better and faster decisions through real-time reports.

- **Automate manual tasks and optimize workflows.** Categorizing transactions from bank accounts and credit cards, sending invoice reminders, and similar daily tasks can be automated programmatically or with AI.

- **Integrate with other systems.** Connect sales, payment, bank, and credit card systems so transactions sync automatically into the accounting backbone. This reduces the effort required to close the books and improves the accuracy of financial records.

- **Support both self-hosted and cloud accounting.** Let users host their data on their own servers and build additional features and integrations on top of the core product. Bigcapital Cloud offers the convenience of accessing financial data without worrying about infrastructure setup.
