> **From the archive:** This post was published in May 2024. The Early Adopter Plan is no longer available.

A few days ago we launched the **Bigcapital Cloud** service and the early adopter plan, with the goal of attracting the first group of beta users who are willing to collaborate and help refine the product. Since we want Bigcapital to be a highly community-driven product, we were looking for 200 early adopters to give us deeper feedback and feature requests, so the product could better serve the market.

Being an early adopter has no specific requirement, such as being active in the community or contributing to the codebase. You can also use the free, self-hosted Bigcapital Community Edition with no obligation. But we believe that since you subscribed early, you are part of the early adopter group, and your feedback and feature requests will always be a priority.

## The Early Adopter Plan includes

- Access to all features of all plans.
- The ability to create three additional organization accounts under the same email.
- Priority support on Discord and email.
- A **$29 one-time** payment for a year.

*The Early Adopter Plan was replaced by a monthly plan once the 200 seats ran out. Subscribed users kept their privileges and support for a year.*
